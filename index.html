The 3 pillars of SEO

Technical, On-Page and Off-Page: the 5 things that matter most in each

Every point shows what it is, how I do it, the tool I use and the result it drives. Open any card for the detail.

Technical SEO = the foundation. If search engines can't crawl, render and index a page quickly, no content or link will help it rank.

  1. 1Crawlability & site architectureMake every important page reachable for Googlebot

    How I do it

    • Full crawl in Screaming Frog to find broken links (4xx), server errors (5xx), redirect chains and orphan pages.
    • Keep key pages within 3 clicks of the homepage with a clean category structure.
    • Fix crawl traps: endless filters, parameters and duplicate paths.

    ToolsScreaming Frog · Search Console

    ResultCrawl budget goes to pages that earn money.

  2. 2Indexation controlThe right pages in Google, the wrong ones out

    How I do it

    • Review the Search Console Pages report: "Crawled – not indexed", "Duplicate without canonical", excluded pages.
    • Clean XML sitemaps and robots.txt; correct canonical and noindex tags.
    • Merge or redirect thin and duplicate pages.

    ToolsSearch Console · Screaming Frog

    ResultMore valuable pages indexed, no index bloat.

  3. 3Page speed & Core Web VitalsLCP, INP and CLS in the green

    How I do it

    • Check the Core Web Vitals report and PageSpeed Insights for slow templates.
    • Compress and convert images (WebP), lazy-load below the fold, set image sizes to stop layout shift.
    • Reduce heavy plugins/apps on WordPress and Shopify, enable caching and minification with the dev team.

    ToolsSearch Console · PageSpeed Insights

    ResultFaster pages, better rankings and conversions.

  4. 4Structured data (schema)Help Google understand and show rich results

    How I do it

    • Add JSON-LD for Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, Article, FAQ and Breadcrumb.
    • Validate with the Rich Results Test and fix errors in Search Console enhancements.
    • Schema also feeds AEO and GEO: it tells machines exactly who and what a page is about.

    ToolsRich Results Test · Search Console

    ResultStars, FAQs and breadcrumbs in results = higher CTR.

  5. 5Mobile-first, HTTPS & redirectsA healthy, secure site on every device

    How I do it

    • Google indexes the mobile version first, so I test layouts, tap targets and content parity on mobile.
    • Full HTTPS, no mixed content; one preferred domain (www or non-www).
    • Plan 301 redirects for removed or moved URLs and fix 404s that have backlinks.

    ToolsScreaming Frog · Search Console

    ResultNo lost link equity, no lost traffic after changes.

On-Page SEO = relevance. Each page targets the right keyword and intent and answers it better than the competition. This work improved my average page rankings by 30%.

  1. 1Keyword research & intent mappingOne main keyword per page, matched to intent

    How I do it

    • Find keywords with volume, difficulty and business value in Semrush, Ahrefs and Keyword Planner.
    • Sort by intent: informational, commercial, transactional, navigational.
    • Build a keyword-to-page map so two pages never compete for the same term (no cannibalisation).

    ToolsSemrush · Ahrefs · Keyword Planner

    ResultEvery page has a clear job and target.

  2. 2Title tags & meta descriptionsWin the click, not just the ranking

    How I do it

    • Titles ~50–60 characters with the main keyword near the start, unique on every page.
    • Meta descriptions ~150–160 characters with a benefit and a call to action.
    • Use Search Console to find pages with high impressions but low CTR, then rewrite them.

    ToolsSearch Console · Screaming Frog

    ResultHigher click-through rate from the same rankings.

  3. 3Headings & content optimisationBetter, clearer, more complete than page 1

    How I do it

    • One clear H1, logical H2/H3 sections that cover the topic fully.
    • Compare the top-ranking pages to find content gaps; brief writers with keywords, questions and structure.
    • Show E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, author info, sources, fresh updates.

    ToolsSemrush · Ahrefs · WordPress/Shopify

    ResultContent that ranks and keeps readers on the page.

  4. 4URL structure & internal linkingPass authority to the pages that matter

    How I do it

    • Short, readable URLs with the keyword (/seo-services-noida, not /page?id=82).
    • Topic clusters: a pillar page linked to and from its supporting articles.
    • Descriptive anchor text and links from strong pages to priority pages.

    ToolsScreaming Frog · Ahrefs

    ResultFaster indexing and stronger key pages.

  5. 5Images, media & user experienceOptimised visuals and readable pages

    How I do it

    • Descriptive file names and alt text, compressed images, image sitemaps where useful.
    • Short paragraphs, lists, tables and clear CTAs for easy reading.
    • Check engagement in GA4 (engagement rate, scroll, conversions) and improve weak pages.

    ToolsGA4 · Canva · Search Console

    ResultImage search traffic and better engagement.

Off-Page SEO = authority and trust. Links and mentions from relevant websites tell Google a site deserves to rank. I've built 200+ quality backlinks.

  1. 1Guest postingRelevant articles on real, niche websites

    How I do it

    • Shortlist sites in the same niche and check their authority (DR/DA), real organic traffic and spam signals in Ahrefs and Semrush.
    • Pitch useful topics and place a contextual link to the right target page.
    • Natural anchor text mix: brand, URL, partial-match.

    ToolsAhrefs · Semrush

    ResultAuthority from sites Google trusts.

  2. 2Niche edits & email outreachLinks inside existing, already-ranking articles

    How I do it

    • Find articles that already rank and get traffic, and request a relevant link insertion.
    • Personalised outreach emails with follow-ups; track every prospect in a sheet.
    • Build long-term relationships with site owners and bloggers.

    ToolsAhrefs · Semrush · Gmail/Sheets

    ResultLinks with immediate value and traffic.

  3. 3Citations & submissionsConsistent presence across directories and platforms

    How I do it

    • Business listings with the same name, address and phone (NAP) for local SEO.
    • Article, PDF, image and classified submissions on quality platforms (started this in my internship).
    • Avoid spammy, low-quality directories.

    ToolsSemrush Listing · Google Sheets

    ResultStronger local trust and brand signals.

  4. 4Competitor backlink gapFind links competitors have and you don't

    How I do it

    • Ahrefs Link Intersect and Semrush Backlink Gap to list sites linking to competitors.
    • Prioritise by relevance and authority, then target them with outreach.
    • Track new and lost links every month.

    ToolsAhrefs · Semrush

    ResultA clear, prioritised link-building plan.

  5. 5Backlink audit & brand mentionsKeep the link profile clean and grow mentions

    How I do it

    • Audit the profile for toxic or spammy links; disavow only when there's a real risk.
    • Turn unlinked brand mentions into links.
    • Brand mentions also help GEO: AI tools trust brands that are talked about across the web.

    ToolsAhrefs · Semrush · Search Console

    ResultA safe, natural, growing link profile.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimisation

Goal: be the direct answer in featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice search.

  • A clear 40–60 word answer right under each question
  • Lists and tables Google can lift into a snippet

GEO

Generative Engine Optimisation

Goal: get the brand cited in AI answers: Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity.

  • Clear entity info: who, what, where (schema + consistent profiles)
  • Original facts, numbers and expert quotes AI can cite
  • Well-structured, up-to-date, easy-to-quote content
  • Brand mentions on trusted sites, reviews and forums
